Notice

Privacy Policy — KhamaKey Moments

Last updated: 21 July 2026. This notice describes how we process personal data when you use KhamaKey Moments (app, account and public NFC pages). It is a translation of the Italian original; if the two texts differ, the Italian version prevails.

1. Data controller

The KhamaKey Moments service is operated by KhamaKey (the service brand available at khamakey.it and app.khamakeymoments.com).

For privacy requests you can use support from the Moments account area or write via the contact channels listed on khamakey.it. Full company details (legal name, registered office, VAT number) must be added here by the controller before final commercial launch.

2. What data we process

  • Account: email, password (hash managed by Supabase Auth), name, linked NFC products.
  • Moments page content: texts, photos, videos, audio, PDFs and settings you upload (e.g. page PIN, letter-to-the-future date).
  • Public interactions: RSVP replies and guestbook messages (if enabled), including data guests enter in the form.
  • Support: the content of emails you send us (e.g. to info@khamakeymoments.com).
  • Technical data: IP address and user-agent used server-side for security (PIN rate limits, abuse prevention) as a hashed identifier; infrastructure logs from Cloudflare/Supabase.
  • Physical product orders: if you buy on Shopify, order data are also processed by Shopify under their notices.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Providing the service (account creation, code activation, editor, public page, plans) — performance of the contract.
  • Security and abuse prevention (PIN, rate limits, upload protection) — legitimate interest / security obligation.
  • Customer support — performance of the contract / legitimate interest.
  • Operational emails (e.g. password reset, service notices via Resend) — performance of the contract.
  • Legal obligations where applicable.

We do not use third-party marketing cookies on public Moments pages. Any technical browser cookies/storage (Auth session, “I have read the privacy notice” preference) are for operation or to remember your choice.

4. Where data are processed (providers)

  • Supabase — database, authentication, any legacy storage.
  • Cloudflare — Pages hosting, Worker ( `/m/` pages, R2 media CDN).
  • Resend — transactional / support email.
  • Shopify — sale of the physical Moments product (if you buy from the store).
  • Stripe — only if you activate a paid Moments plan (when connected).

Providers may process data in the EU or in third countries with appropriate safeguards (e.g. standard contractual clauses), according to their policies.

5. Retention

  • Account data and page content: for the life of the relationship and then for as long as needed for legal duties or to close deletion requests.
  • Support tickets: for as long as useful to handle support.
  • Security / rate-limit logs: short, technical retention.
  • Media on R2: while they remain linked to your page or until you remove them / the account is closed under the service procedures.

6. Recipients and public pages

If you publish a Moments page, the content you make public is accessible to anyone with the NFC link (and, if enabled, the PIN). RSVP and guestbook, if enabled, collect data from invited third parties: you are responsible for informing them adequately when you share the page.

7. Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability within the limits of the law, and lodge a complaint with the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (Italian Data Protection Authority). To exercise your rights use Moments support or KhamaKey contacts.

8. Minors

The service is intended for adults or anyone with capacity to accept the terms. If you believe a minor’s data were uploaded without the required conditions, contact us for removal.

9. Updates

We may update this notice: the date at the top of the page indicates the current version.
